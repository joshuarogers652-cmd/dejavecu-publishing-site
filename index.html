The EMMA Series

Some memories transcend time. Some connections transcend lifetimes. Some choices change everything.

Four books. One extraordinary mystery. A love that challenges the boundaries of time.

What if the life you’re living isn’t the first time you’ve lived it?

Nineteen-year-old Emma, a forensic psychology student at St. John’s University in Queens, New York, begins experiencing an increasingly disturbing phenomenon: memories of conversations that haven’t happened, places she has never visited, and moments that feel impossibly familiar.

What initially appears to be déjà vu soon develops into something far more extraordinary—déjà vécu, the sensation of having already lived an experience.

When Emma meets Ethan, their inexplicable connection draws them into a mystery that challenges the very foundations of memory, identity, consciousness, and time. Together, they uncover hidden research, forgotten histories, family secrets, and unsettling evidence suggesting that their lives may be connected in ways neither could have imagined.

Across four interconnected novels, Emma’s search for answers transforms into a deeply personal journey through the mysteries of human existence, the consequences of choice, and the enduring power of love.

J.P. Rogers

J.P. Rogers is the author of the EMMA series, a four-book psychological thriller series inspired by a simple moment that became an unforgettable question.

The idea for EMMA began during a conversation with his daughter after she made a small, almost unconscious movement. When he asked why she had done it, she smiled and explained that she sometimes changes what she's doing whenever she experiences déjà vu — hoping to alter whatever was supposed to happen next. That conversation sparked a question that refused to let go: What if déjà vu isn't just a feeling of familiarity? What if it's a memory of something already lived?

That single idea grew into a story exploring memory, identity, love, sacrifice, and the unseen moments that shape our lives.

Drawing from a career leading mission-critical operations and complex facilities, J.P. brings a deep appreciation for precision, leadership, and human resilience to his writing. His fiction blends psychological mystery with grounded realism, inviting readers to question the nature of memory and the possibility that some experiences can't be explained by coincidence alone.

J.P. lives in Texas with his family, where he continues to explore the extraordinary questions hidden within ordinary moments.
