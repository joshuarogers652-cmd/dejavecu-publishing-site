The Imprint

Stories built to recur.

Déjà Vécu Publishing exists for the moments that don't announce themselves the first time — fiction that trusts a reader to sit with a feeling before they understand it. We publish psychological, patient, character-driven stories about memory, inheritance, and the things that come back around. Based in Houston, Texas.

Book I of the Déjà Vécu Trilogy

EMMA Déjà Vécu

A Novel by Joshua P. Rogers

Nineteen-year-old Emma Rogers has always known things before they happen — not premonitions, exactly. Recurrences. A conversation she's certain she's already had. A stranger's face that arrives pre-familiar. At St. John's University in Queens, she's learned to call it déjà vu and let it pass.

Then a psychology professor with an unfinished study, a father who has spent years disappearing into an alias, and a phenomenon with no name in any textbook converge on the same impossible conclusion: Emma isn't imagining the recurrence. She's inside one — and the deeper she goes, the more the timeline resists letting her go without a cost.

EMMA: Déjà Vécu is a psychological thriller about memory, inheritance, and the echoes we don't outrun.
